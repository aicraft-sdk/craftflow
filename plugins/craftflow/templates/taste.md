# Taste

What this team wants gone from its interface, and what it keeps on purpose. Copy it to
`.craftflow/state/project/taste.md`; `frontend-design-critique` reads it on every critique. Adapted from unslop-ui's
taste example (MIT, see `NOTICE`).

## Bans

Each bullet adds one tell. The first three are also counted by `scripts/craftflow_ui_tells.py` as T1, T2 and T3; the
critic reads the rest.

- Exclamation marks
- Uppercase text, not only eyebrows
- Middle dots between items
- Emoji, including in docs
- The word "seamless"

## Keep

Reported under "Kept on purpose" and never flagged, whatever a rule says.

- The Beta badge in the header
- Legal text in the footer
